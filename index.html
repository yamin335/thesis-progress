    Synthetic data

    The capture matrix on the KIVI_Twin digital twin and the three YOLO datasets exported from it: kt_v1 (vehicles by type), kt_v1p (the same frames with the classes parked vehicle and other vehicle) and kt_v2p (plus the runs with traffic around the camera car). Split is by run, never by frame; road 2 appears only in the test split. The cards and charts at the top describe kt_v1.

    Training

    Every training run of the thesis, grouped by stage: the fine-tuning on the synthetic dataset (S1), the ablation with every layer trained, the parked class of EXP-L and the further round of EXP-N. Each stage states its dataset and its validation set. Validation figures compare within a stage only, because the stages are validated on different data. A run in progress is shown as running, and its curve grows with every republished epoch.

    Experiments and results
    The experiments EXP-A to EXP-N, one group each. EXP-A to EXP-J were fixed in the experiment plan before the work, EXP-K to EXP-N were added in November 2026, each with a protocol written before its test. A group holds a short overview, tables with a note on what the columns mean, charts, figures, and where they exist the result rows of all_results.csv and the per-row records. EXP-G and EXP-H needed real footage and were not carried out. The result files as they were written follow at the end.
